RUN Voxel Packs — Fantasy avatar rig files (parts, skins, clips).
These files carry the Pirate Nation avatar armature (joint names and bind
pose, from https://github.com/proofofplay/piratenation-game) so that they bind
to Pirate Nation avatars. They carry no Pirate Nation meshes or clips.
